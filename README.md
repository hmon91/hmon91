### Hi, I'm Hamidreza Montazeri Hedesh 👋

I'm a PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at [Northeastern University](https://www.northeastern.edu/), working in [Prof. Milad Siami's lab](LAB_URL) on **control theory** and **safe learning-based control**.

My research develops certificates that guarantee the **stability** and **positivity** of dynamical systems driven by **neural-network controllers** — using tools such as sector-bounded nonlinearities, Metzler/Hurwitz conditions, and positive-systems theory to make learned controllers provably safe.

---

#### Research Interests

- Stability and safety guarantees for neural-network controllers
- Positive systems and Metzler / Hurwitz analysis
- Sector-bounded nonlinearities and absolute stability
- Learning-based and data-driven control
- Robust and constrained control
